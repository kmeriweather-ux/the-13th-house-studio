The Studio

The Rooms

The 13th House Studio is organized into thirteen rooms — each a distinct territory of creative practice. Some hold language. Some hold sound, image, and motion. Some hold the long relationships between a book and the people who carry it. All of them open slowly.

Room I

The Bookstore Room

Books, journals, and long-form editorial work. The room where language slows down. Home to The 13th Chapter and the Evidence Series — future editions, pocket volumes, and poetry collections grow from this shelf.

Room II

The Mic Within

Spoken word, voice essays, and quiet audio. The interior made audible. Look here for readings from the book, seasonal monologues, and conversations about the work of noticing.

Room III

The Motion Picture Room

Film, reels, and visual storytelling. Short pieces that sit somewhere between an editorial and a mood. A moving archive of the studio\u2019s world.

Room IV

Beauty4U2

Beauty and wellness, considered. The quiet rituals — skin, scent, light — that return us to our bodies. Curated products, essays, and partnerships the studio chooses carefully.

Room V

Music Room

Compositions, curated playlists, and soundtracks for the reader. Scores for stillness. Scores for weather. A room meant for listening, not performing.

Room VI

Art Gallery

Visual art, botanical studies, and the imagery of the studio. Original pieces, gallery prints, and the slow accumulation of a visual vocabulary — cream, gold, and the particular light of September.

Room VII

Community Room

A quiet gathering place for readers who stay. Circles, small correspondences, and the long relationship between a book and the people who carry it forward.

Room VIII

App Lab

Digital tools built for attention, not distraction. Small, considered technologies that help a reader return to themselves — companions to the practice, not replacements for it.

Room IX

University of Me

Courses, mentorships, and learning programs in the discipline of paying attention. Structured study for readers who want to work deeper into the framework the book introduces.

Room X

The Stage Room

Live readings, talks, and in-person gatherings. When the studio steps out from behind the page and sits with people in a room.

Room XI

The Brand Studio

The voice, the look, and the language the studio uses to meet the world. Campaigns, collaborations, and the editorial identity behind each edition.

Room XII

The Business Office

Editions, partnerships, and the practical architecture of the studio. The room where the work meets the commerce that sustains it — chosen with the same care as the writing itself.

Room XIII

The Vault

The studio\u2019s long memory. A private archive of master works, past editions, and the writing that informs what comes next. Occasionally, something is brought up from The Vault and shared.

Thirteen rooms. One unhurried practice of paying attention.
